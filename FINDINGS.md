# Investigation Findings

## Executive conclusion

I found that stable revenue masks a substantial decline in contribution margin. The evidence points to three overlapping drivers: increased discounting at Coastal Hub, higher operating costs at South Hub, and a higher return rate within the Recovery category.

## Recommended actions

1. Review Coastal Hub discount approvals and measure incremental volume against margin lost.
2. Break South Hub's operating-cost increase into labor, facility, and service components.
3. Inspect Recovery-product return reasons and suppliers before changing assortment decisions.
4. Track net revenue, contribution, average discount, and return rate together rather than reviewing revenue alone.
